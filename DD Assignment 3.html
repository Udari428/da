R1 — Cipher descriptions

  • Vigenère: A repeating keyword shifts letters by changing amounts. This tool preserves case and punctuation and advances the key on letters only.
  • AES-256-GCM: Symmetric encryption with a 256-bit key. It authenticates the message, so a wrong key or modified ciphertext causes decryption to fail.
  • ROT13: A Caesar cipher that rotates letters by 13 places. It is reversible but is not secure encryption.
